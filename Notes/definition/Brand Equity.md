# Brand Equity

* **Definition:** The asset value and magnetic pull of an access handler, reflecting its capacity to attract consumers, command price premiums, and drive firm profitability [L7 Slide 15, Slide 17].
* **BrandDynamics Metrics:**
  * **Power:** Volume share driven by preference [L7 Slide 17].
  * **Premium:** Price index advantage (Brand Price / Category Avg Price) [L7 Slide 17].
  * **Potential:** Probability of value share growth [L7 Slide 17].
