# MSE Framework

* **Definition:** A design philosophy that requires solution architects to coordinate three core elements simultaneously when building any consumer solution [L4 Slide 25, 62].
* **Components:**
  * **[[Market]]**: The target cognitive and affect states the solution must activate in the consumer.
  * **[[Solution]]**: The functional and structural configuration of the product being delivered.
  * **Experience**: The resulting emotional affect state (hedonic realization) felt by the consumer during and after consumption.
* **Purpose:** Ensures that every design decision serves a specific market state, is coherently delivered through the solution, and results in a positive emotional experience — preventing misalignment between technical features and human psychological needs.
