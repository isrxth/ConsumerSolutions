# Market

* **Definition:** A collection of similar cognitive and affect states, rather than a physical location or demographic segment [Rules.md, L3 Slide 16].
* **Dynamic Realization:** Markets are dynamically constructed and deconstructed with every single consumption cycle (e.g., every bite of a pizza realizes a market, and the next is driven by a newly realized state) [Rules.md, L3 Slide 18-19].
