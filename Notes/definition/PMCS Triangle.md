# PMCS Triangle

* **Definition:** A foundational framework in Consumer Solution Designing that maps the three essential components of any consumer solution: the **Consumer** (who has the pain), the **Product** (the medium/vessel that delivers the solution), and the **Solution** (the interactive experience that resolves the pain) [L1 Slide 2].
* **Components:**
  * **[[Consumer]] ("Who")**: The individual with dynamic psychological and physical pains.
  * **[[Product]] ("Through")**: The signal container through which the solution is delivered.
  * **[[Solution]] ("What")**: The value-resolving experience itself.
* **Purpose:** Ensures solution architects consider all three dimensions before writing code or designing systems.
