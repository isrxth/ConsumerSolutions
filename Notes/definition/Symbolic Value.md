# Symbolic Value

* **Definition:** The psychological and sociocultural meaning associated with a product [Rules.md, L4 Slide 16].
* **Sub-categories:**
  * **Personal Meaning:** Self-expression and personal history (e.g., "my mom used this") [L4 Slide 16].
  * **Social Meaning:** Social prestige and group identity ("what society thinks of me") [L4 Slide 16].
  * **Conditional/Socio-cultural Meaning:** Contextual meanings driven by ethnic or cultural factors [L4 Slide 16].
