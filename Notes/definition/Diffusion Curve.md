# Diffusion Curve

* **Definition:** A distribution curve categorizing adopters based on cognitive risk tolerance and technological receptivity [L6 Slide 27].
* **Adopter Tiers:**
  * **Innovators:** High risk tolerance, well-resourced, driven by novelty [L6 Slide 27].
  * **Early Adopters:** Educated opinion leaders and technology advocates seeking competitive edge [L6 Slide 27].
  * **Early Majority:** Risk-averse pragmatists requiring proven stability and peer validation [L6 Slide 27].
  * **Late Majority:** Skeptical consumers adopting only when the solution becomes mainstream standard [L6 Slide 27].
  * **Laggards:** Traditionalists adopting exclusively when former alternatives are obsolete [L6 Slide 27].
