# Kano Model

* **Definition:** An analytical model categorizing product attributes based on how their presence and performance influence customer satisfaction and [[Psychological Equilibrium]] [L6 Slide 6].
* **Attribute Types:**
  * **Basic Attributes (Musts):** Baseline requirements; presence does not create satisfaction, but absence causes intense dissatisfaction (e.g., bug-free OS) [L6 Slide 6]. Maps to [[Enabler]]s.
  * **Performance Attributes (Linear):** Satisfaction is directly proportional to attribute execution; consumers willingly pay more for higher performance (e.g., processor speed) [L6 Slide 6].
  * **Excitement Attributes (Delighters):** Unexpected, highly noticeable innovations; presence produces customer delight, while absence does not cause dissatisfaction (e.g., voice OS commands) [L6 Slide 6]. Maps to [[Differentiator]]s and [[Augmenter]]s.
