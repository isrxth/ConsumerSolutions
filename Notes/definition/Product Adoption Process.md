# Product Adoption Process

* **Definition:** The sequential micro-stages through which an individual consumer passes from initial exposure to full mental and behavioral commitment to a solution [L6 Slide 26].
* **Stages:** Awareness -> Interest -> Evaluation -> Trial -> Adoption [L6 Slide 26].
* **Rate Factors:** Modulated by relative advantage, compatibility, complexity, divisibility, and communicability [L6 Slide 29].
