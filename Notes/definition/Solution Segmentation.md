# Solution Segmentation

* **Definition:** Dividing a market into distinct cognitive and affect states, pain profiles, or behavioral requirements that necessitate differentiated solution compositions or marketing mixes [Rules.md, L5 Slide 4].
* **Computational Core:** Segments represent splits in multidimensional cognitive space coinciding with differential consumption cycles (pre, on, post) [L5 Slide 8, Slide 13].
* **Core Question:** "What pains am I providing a solution for, and how many consumers will give 'YES' to this solution?" [L5 Slide 6, Slide 7].
