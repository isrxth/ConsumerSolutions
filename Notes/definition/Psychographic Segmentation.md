# Psychographic Segmentation

* **Definition:** Dividing a market based on how individuals express their inner self, including social class, lifestyle, and personality traits [L5 Slide 13, Slide 19].
* **Expression Mechanism:** Products serve as symbolic signal containers for self-expression and identity projection (resolving [[Self-worth]]) [Rules.md, L5 Slide 19, Slide 20]. E.g., Harley-Davidson sells a lifestyle, anchored on high mechanical performance [L5 Slide 20].
