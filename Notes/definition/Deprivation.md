# Deprivation

* **Definition:** The felt psychological dissonance or tension caused by the gap/inconsistency between cognitions defining the consumer's "current state" and those defining their [[Psychological Equilibrium]] [Rules.md, L3 Slide 8].
* **Role in Consumption:** It is the primary engine of consumption, representing the acute mental or biological pain that requires a solution [L3 Slide 8, Slide 21].
