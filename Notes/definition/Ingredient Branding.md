# Ingredient Branding

* **Definition:** Leveraging an established subordinate brand within a parent solution (e.g., "Intel Inside", "Energy Star") to borrow trust, technical credibility, and positive associations [L7 Slide 24, Slide 25].
* **Psychological Function:** Directly reduces perceived risk and cognitive uncertainty, lowering [[Sacrifice Value|psychic sacrifice]] [L7 Slide 24].
