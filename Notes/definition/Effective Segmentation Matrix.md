# Effective Segmentation Matrix

* **Definition:** An evaluative metric (MASDA) ensuring market segments are viable for solution design and commercial deployment [L5 Slide 23].
* **Criteria:**
  * **Measurable:** Size, purchasing power, and cognitive profiles can be quantified [L5 Slide 23].
  * **Accessible:** Visible contact points exist to transmit stimuli and interact with the segment [L5 Slide 23].
  * **Substantial:** Segments are sufficiently large or profitable to warrant dedicated solution architecture [L5 Slide 23].
  * **Differential:** Segments respond distinctively to specific solution stimuli (if responses are identical, group them together) [L5 Slide 23].
  * **Actionable:** The firm possesses the resources and technical capabilities to execute programs for the segment [L5 Slide 23].
