# Behavioral Segmentation

* **Definition:** Dividing consumers based on their knowledge of, attitude toward, use of, or response to a solution [L5 Slide 13, Slide 21].
* **Dimensions:** Includes occasion segmentation (consumption moments), benefit sought (value priorities, e.g., Standard vs. Enterprise), usage status (non-users, new, current, ex-users), and usage rate [L5 Slide 21, Slide 22].
