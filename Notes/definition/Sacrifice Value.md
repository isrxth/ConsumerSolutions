# Sacrifice Value

* **Definition:** Minimizing the total cognitive, biological, and economic costs of consumption [Rules.md, L4 Slide 17].
* **Dimensions:**
  * **Monetary Cost:** Pre-purchase, purchase, use, and post-use monetary expenses [L4 Slide 17].
  * **Energy/Effort Cost:** The biological or physical work required to consume [L4 Slide 17].
  * **Psychic Cost:** The stress, anxiety, and perceived risk associated with the solution [Rules.md, L4 Slide 17].
  * **Time Cost:** The time invested in obtaining and using the product [L4 Slide 17].
