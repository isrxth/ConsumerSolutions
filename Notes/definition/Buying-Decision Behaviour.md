# Buying-Decision Behaviour

* **Definition:** A 2x2 matrix categorizing consumer decision dynamics based on degree of buyer involvement (high vs. low) and perceived differences between brands (significant vs. few) [L6 Slide 22].
* **Classifications:**
  * **Complex Buying Behaviour:** High involvement + Significant brand differences (e.g., purchasing a workstation laptop) [L6 Slide 22].
  * **Dissonance Reducing Buying Behaviour:** High involvement + Few perceived brand differences; high post-purchase tension (e.g., 4K monitor) [L6 Slide 22].
  * **Variety Seeking Buying Behaviour:** Low involvement + Significant brand differences; frequent switching driven by boredom or hedonic curiosity (e.g., video games) [L6 Slide 22].
  * **Habitual Buying Behaviour:** Low involvement + Few brand differences; unconscious automatic selection (e.g., default web browser) [L6 Slide 22].
