# Enabler

* **Definition:** Primary value elements that keep the solution viable. They are the reasons why consumers "consider" a solution and define the generic industry standard [Rules.md, L4 Slide 31].
* **Characteristics:** Must be fully maintained, but they are often not actively communicated in marketing since they are assumed (e.g., Rolex keeping accurate time, Boeing flying safely) [L4 Slide 31].
