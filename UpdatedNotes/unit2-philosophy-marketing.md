# Unit 2: Philosophy of Marketing & Value Co-Creation (L2)

This unit shifts marketing from a set of functional tasks to an **attitude of mind** [9]. It moves away from selling physical products to managing active consumer experiences and co-creating value through [[Service-Dominant (S-D) Logic]] [10, 11, 12].

---

## 1. Core Definitions

*   **Marketing (Modern)**: Understanding [[Consumer|consumers]], designing [[Solution|consumer solutions]], and managing them to build profitable relationships by creating and growing markets [7].
*   **A Consumer Problem**: A state where a consumer has **pains in their mind** — an active state of [[Deprivation]] [6]. These psychological, physical, or social pains are what trigger [[Consumption]] [6].
*   **Value-in-Exchange vs. Value-in-Use**:
    *   **Value-in-Exchange**: The monetary price paid for a product during a transaction — the monetary component of [[Sacrifice Value]] [10].
    *   **Value-in-Use**: The actual [[Instrumental Value|value]] realized and felt *during the consumption and active interaction* with the [[Product]] [10]. This is the core of [[Service-Dominant (S-D) Logic]].

---

## 2. G-D Logic vs. S-D Logic
The ultimate philosophical shift in modern business is moving from [[Goods-Dominant (G-D) Logic]] to [[Service-Dominant (S-D) Logic]] [10]:

| Dimension | [[Goods-Dominant (G-D) Logic]] | [[Service-Dominant (S-D) Logic]] |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Concept** | Value is **embedded inside physical goods** [10, 11]. | Everything is a **service**; physical goods are merely facilitators of services [10]. |
| **Focus** | Tangible assets, transactions, and standardized products [10]. | Intangibles: knowledge, specialized skills, relationships, and experiences [10]. |
| **Value Creation** | Created solely by the firm and distributed to consumers [11]. | **Co-created** dynamically through active interaction with the consumer — see [[Value Co-creation]] [10, 11]. |
| **Role of Firm** | Proprietary creator of value [11]. | [[Enabler]] and resource provider [11]. |

---

## 3. The Dynamic of Value Co-Creation

*   **Active Consumer Involvement**: [[Value Co-creation]] means getting consumers involved in the ideation, customization, and active use of a solution [11]. Consumers excel at taking an existing idea and developing it further, rather than inventing something entirely brand new [11].
*   **Incremental Cycles of Value**: Value realization is not a single, instantaneous event. It is **incremental and experience-bound** [12]. One set of values must be realized first to set the stage for the next set [12].
    *   *Why this matters*: This is why consumers often cannot tell you what they want or need in advance—they must experience the initial stages of value first [12]. This is the core logic behind [[Augmenter]]s in the [[AVP Framework]].
*   **Ecosystemic Marketing**: [[Consumption]] occurs within the [[Value Delivery System]] — a complex, multi-layered ecosystem (consumers, employees, natural and social environments) [13]. True marketing requires protecting the well-being of the [[Consumer]] by protecting this entire ecosystem [13].

## 4. Practical Examples & Neuromarketing Alignments

### 🧠 Neuromarketing Reference: Expectation vs. Neural Reality
"What we manage is not the relationship, but the consumer's **expectations and feelings**." [12]
In neuroscience, the brain acts as a **prediction engine**. It constantly builds mental models (cognitive schemas) and anticipates rewards [6].
*   **Dopamine Spike**: Dopamine is released in anticipation of a reward, not just the reward itself. This relates directly to managing [[Psychological Equilibrium]].
*   **Prediction Error**: If a software application crashes or fails to deliver on its expectation, it triggers a negative prediction error, releasing stress hormones (cortisol) and causing psychic pain — a form of [[Sacrifice Value|psychic sacrifice cost]]. Designing solutions is about aligning sensory cues to maintain positive neural expectations.

### 🌟 Real-World Example: Lego Ideas & Customization
*   **Traditional ([[Goods-Dominant (G-D) Logic|G-D]]) Approach**: Lego designs a toy, manufactures it, and sells it in a box. Value is embedded in the physical plastic bricks [10, 11].
*   **Co-Creation ([[Service-Dominant (S-D) Logic|S-D]]) Approach (Lego Ideas)**: Lego hosts an online platform where consumers design their own sets, vote on other users' submissions, and collaborate on adjustments [11]. The consumer actively co-creates the experience [11]. Lego acts as an [[Enabler]] and resource provider, turning the physical toy into an ongoing interactive service ecosystem — a [[Value Delivery System]] [10, 11].
